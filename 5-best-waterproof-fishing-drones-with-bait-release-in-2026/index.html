Common Mistakes to Avoid

In our research, we have seen countless anglers make avoidable mistakes that lead to broken gear and lost fish. Watch out for these three major pitfalls:

  1. Confusing Water-Resistant with Waterproof: Never fly a standard "weather-resistant" consumer drone over saltwater. Ocean spray, high humidity, and minor splashes will quickly corrode internal circuit boards and seize brushless motors.
  2. Ignoring Post-Flight Decontamination: Failing to rinse your drone in clean fresh water immediately after a saltwater session will cause salt crystals to dry inside the motor bells, destroying the bearings within weeks.
  3. Overloading the Payload Limits: Pushing a drone past its maximum stated payload capacity drastically reduces battery efficiency, lowers wind resistance, and dramatically increases the risk of a catastrophic mid-air motor cutoff.
