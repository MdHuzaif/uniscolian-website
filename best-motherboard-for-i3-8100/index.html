GIGABYTE H310M A 2.0

The GIGABYTE H310M A gaming motherboard is designed for gaming computers that need to be both powerful and reasonably priced. It supports Core i3 8100 processors manufactured by Intel in the last three generations (Intel® Coffee Lake, Kaby Lake Refresh, and Skylake). The board offers dual-channel memory of up to 2400 MHz with non-ECC memory modules. The gaming motherboard also offers a variety of ports, such as two USB Type C (USB-C) ports, four PCIe Gen II x16 slots, and three SATA III connectors for hard drives or SSDs with speeds up to 128 GB/s.

The features that provide the most flexibility are probably its Smart Fan and RGB LED strips which can be configured to light the motherboard in seven different colors. The gaming board also offers a number of gaming-specific features, such as support for WiFi antennas and M.2 SSDs.

This gaming board is excellent value for gamers who want their computer to last with minimal maintenance costs, but it does not offer overclocking options or SLI compatibility. Overall excellent Core i3 8100 motherboards.

The ASRock Z390 PRO4 motherboard is an excellent choice for a large variety of computer builds, with various features to suit the needs of any gamer or professional. It supports Intel’s latest LGA1151 processors and offers four DDR-DIMM slots that can hold up to 64GBs of memory at speeds up to 3600MHz. With two PCI-Express x1 slots and three PCIe x16 slots, it has plenty of room for adding additional graphics cards as well.

The ASRock Z390 PRO4 is a gaming motherboard. It offers the latest Intel Core i3-8100 CPUs and up to 128GB of DDR4 RAM for gaming systems with high demands on performance, as well as storage space for large files thanks to its eight SATA III ports and two M.2 slot slots. The board also has an analog audio connector for gaming headsets, and a clear CMOS jumper to help with BIOS settings.

The ASRock Z390 PRO4 features onboard 802.11ac Wi-Fi so gamers who want a wireless connection will not have any problems with the device, as well as USB Type C ports which are perfect for connecting devices such as gaming headsets.

MSI B360 GAMING PLUS

The gaming plus series from MSI has a handful of gaming motherboards that are targeted at budget gamers. The B360 GAMING PLUS is one of the boards in this lineup and it’s very affordable for its class. In terms of features, it has all the standard gaming fare such as an RGB LED logo and gaming ports, along with some extras like a second M.2 SATA slot (M key) for expansion or fast storage.

The MSI B360 GAMING PLUS motherboard has been around for a while now and it still remains one of my favorite gaming boards on the market today. It’s always in stock, it has a good gaming experience, and the price is just right. The board graphics are capable of playing most games at reasonable framerates with a gaming card with the right specifications (graphics card).

The MSI B360 GAMING PLUS motherboard features support for up to 64GB DDR-RAM in four slots running at 2400MHz, with a maximum supported speed of 2666MHz. The four slots are positioned in two sets, with the first set near the CPU and the second set located at the other end of this gaming motherboard. All four DDR4-RAM slots on this gaming board feature support for up to 64GB modules per slot, which is great news if you’re considering future upgrades.

There are two PCI-Express x16 slots on the gaming motherboard, with support for either 16x or a maximum of four cards running in an x16 mode (PCIe Gen I/II). The MSI B360 GAMING PLUS also features four PCIe x1 slot, and one M.2SATA port to improve storage. The gaming motherboard is also equipped with two USB-A ports, six USB-B, and one type C as well.

The gaming motherboard features a Realtek ALC8925 codec that provides support for up to 192kHz/24bit audio streams in Windows systems, which allows for true high definition sound quality when gaming or streaming games online.

Is i3 8100 enough for gaming?

The gaming community has been asking the question “Is i3 8100 enough for gaming?” This article will answer that question. The Intel Core i3-8100 is a quad-core processor, with hyperthreading and an L2 cache of 3 MB. It is at the level of a Core i5-7500 as it also features 4 threads with no hyperthreading.

It has a higher base clock speed than the Core i5-7500 but lowers turbo boost frequency. The IPC improvements from this newer generation are also present in the Core i3 processors, which means they should perform better than older models when running single-threaded tasks. Overall, the performance of this CPU is good for gaming even though it has a lower turbo frequency.
